## 🚀 How to Test on iPhone (2 minutes)

### Step 1: Deploy to Vercel
```bash
vercel
```

### Step 2: Open on iPhone
1. Open **Safari**
2. Go to your Vercel URL (e.g., `https://pipduel.vercel.app`)
4. Tap **"Add to Home Screen"**
6. Tap **"Add"**

### Step 3: Test
- App icon appears on home screen
- Tap to open (full-screen, no browser)
- Test all features
- Share with friends!

**That's it!** You now have PipDuel on your iPhone! 🎊
