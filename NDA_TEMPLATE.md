## 1. PURPOSE

---

## 2. DEFINITION OF CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

### 2.1 "Confidential Information"
Shall mean any and all information disclosed by Company to Recipient, whether orally, in binding form, electronically, or by any other means, including but not limited to:

1. **Business Information**
   - Business plans and strategies
   - Financial projections and forecasts
   - Marketing plans and strategies
   - Pricing information
   - Customer lists and data
   - Vendor and supplier information
   - Partnership agreements

2. **Technical Information**
   - Source code and object code
   - Algorithms and logic
   - System architectures
   - Technical specifications
   - API documentation
   - Database schemas
   - Software designs
   - Hardware designs

3. **Product Information**
   - Product designs and prototypes
   - User interface designs
   - User experience flows
   - Product roadmaps
   - Feature specifications
   - Testing procedures

4. **Financial Information**
   - Revenue models
   - Cost structures
   - Investment information
   - Funding sources
   - Valuation information
   - Cap tables

5. **Operational Information**
   - Business processes
   - Operational procedures
   - Quality control methods
   - Training materials
   - Employee information
   - Contractor information

6. **Other Information**
   - Trade secrets
   - Know-how
   - Research and development
   - Intellectual property
   - Patent applications
   - Trademark applications
   - Copyright information
   - Any information marked as "confidential"
   - Any information that should reasonably be understood as confidential

### 2.2 Exclusions
Confidential Information shall not include information that:

1. Is or becomes publicly known through no fault of Recipient
2. Was rightfully known to Recipient prior to disclosure
3. Is rightfully received from a third party without restriction
4. Is independently developed by Recipient without use of Confidential Information
8. Is required to be disclosed by law or court order (provided Recipient gives Company prompt notice)

---

## 3. OBLIGATIONS OF RECEIVING PARTY

### 3.1 Non-Disclosure
Recipient shall hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence and shall not disclose any Confidential Information to any third party without the prior binding consent of Company.

### 3.2 Non-Use
Recipient shall not use any Confidential Information for any purpose other than the Purpose specified in this Agreement.

### 3.3 Protection
Recipient shall take all reasonable precautions to protect the confidentiality of the Confidential Information, including:

1. Using at least the same degree of care as Recipient uses to protect its own confidential information
2. In no event less than reasonable care
4. Limiting access to Confidential Information to employees, agents, or representatives who have a need to know
8. Ensuring such persons are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as restrictive as this Agreement

### 3.4 No Reverse Engineering
Recipient shall not reverse engineer, decompile, or disassemble any software or other products or processes disclosed by Company.

### 3.5 No Copying
Recipient shall not copy or reproduce any Confidential Information except as reasonably necessary for the Purpose.

---

## 4. TERM

### 4.1 Agreement Term
This Agreement shall remain in effect for a period of [2/3/5] years from the Effective Date.

### 4.2 Confidentiality Period
Recipient's obligations with respect to Confidential Information shall survive for a period of [3/5/7] years from the date of disclosure, or indefinitely for trade secrets.

## 6. RETURN OF MATERIALS

Upon termination of discussions regarding the Purpose or upon Company's request, Recipient shall promptly:

1. Return all documents and materials containing Confidential Information
2. Delete all electronic copies of Confidential Information
4. Certify in binding form that all Confidential Information has been returned or destroyed
8. Not retain any copies, summaries, or extracts of Confidential Information

### 8.1 No License

### 8.2 No Warranty
Company makes no warranty, express or implied, regarding the accuracy, completeness, or fitness for a particular purpose of any Confidential Information.

## 10. REMEDIES

Recipient acknowledges that any breach of this Agreement may cause irreparable harm to Company for which monetary damages would be inadequate. Recipient agrees that Company shall be entitled to seek injunctive relief and specific performance in addition to any other remedies available at law or equity.

### 10.2 No Bond Required

### 10.3 Indemnification
