# 🍎🅖 Apple Pay & Google Pay Withdrawal Options Added

## ✅ New Features Added

Apple Pay and Google Pay have been successfully added as withdrawal options in the PipDuel wallet system.

---

## 🎯 Updated Withdrawal Methods

You can now withdraw your winnings using **5 different methods**:

1. 💳 **PayPal** - Withdraw to PayPal account
2. 🍎 **Apple Pay** - Withdraw to Apple Pay (NEW!)
3. 🅖 **Google Pay** - Withdraw to Google Pay (NEW!)
4. 🏦 **Bank Transfer** - Withdraw to bank account
5. ₿ **Cryptocurrency** - Withdraw to crypto wallet (BTC/ETH/USDT)

---

## 🍎 Apple Pay Withdrawal

### How It Works
- Select "Apple Pay" as your withdrawal method
- Enter your Apple ID email or phone number
- Funds will be sent to your Apple Pay account
- Processing time: 1-3 business days

### What You Need
- Apple ID email address OR
- Phone number associated with your Apple Pay account
- Apple Pay must be set up on your device

### Example
```
Withdrawal Amount: $100
Processing Fee (2%): $2
You Receive: $98
Method: Apple Pay
Details: john.apple@example.com
```

## 🔄 Complete Withdrawal Flow

### Step 1: Select Amount
- Choose from quick options: $50, $100, $250, $500
- Or enter custom amount (minimum $10)
- System validates against your balance

### Step 2: Choose Withdrawal Method
Now you have **5 options**:
- 💳 PayPal
- 🍎 Apple Pay (NEW!)
- 🅖 Google Pay (NEW!)
- 🏦 Bank Transfer
- ₿ Cryptocurrency

### Step 3: Enter Account Details
Depending on your chosen method:
- **PayPal**: Enter PayPal email address
- **Apple Pay**: Enter Apple ID email or phone number
- **Google Pay**: Enter Google account email or phone number
- **Bank Transfer**: Enter bank account number
- **Cryptocurrency**: Enter wallet address (BTC/ETH/USDT)

### Step 4: Review & Confirm
- See withdrawal amount
- See 2% processing fee
- See final amount you'll receive
- Confirm your account details
- Click "Confirm Withdrawal"

### Step 5: Processing
- Funds are processed within 1-3 business days
- You'll receive a confirmation message
- Transaction is logged in your history

---

## 💰 Fee Structure

All withdrawal methods have the same fee structure:
- **Processing Fee**: 2% of withdrawal amount
- **Minimum Withdrawal**: $10
- **Processing Time**: 1-3 business days

### Example Calculation
```
Withdrawal Amount: $100
Processing Fee (2%): $2
You Receive: $98
```
