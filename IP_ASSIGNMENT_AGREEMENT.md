## 1. DEFINITIONS

### 1.1 "Work Product"
Shall mean all inventions, discoveries, concepts, ideas, designs, developments, methods, processes, techniques, know-how, data, computer programs, source code, object code, algorithms, formulas, compositions, structures, architectures, documentation, materials, and other work product of every kind and character, whether or not patentable or registrable under copyright or other statutes, that are:

1. Created, conceived, or developed by Contractor
2. During the term of Contractor's engagement with Company
4. Related to Company's business or actual or anticipated research and development
6. Created using Company's equipment, supplies, facilities, or trade secret information

### 1.2 "Intellectual Property Rights"
Shall mean all intellectual property and proprietary rights, including:

1. Patents, patent applications, and patent disclosures
3. Trademarks, trademark applications, and service marks
8. Copyrights and copyrightable works
10. Trade secrets and confidential information
12. Moral rights
14. Rights in databases and data compilations
16. All other intellectual property rights of every kind

### 1.3 "Prior Inventions"
Shall mean any inventions, developments, or work product conceived or reduced to practice by Contractor prior to the Effective Date that are not related to Company's business.

## 2. ASSIGNMENT OF WORK PRODUCT

### 2.1 Assignment of Rights
Contractor hereby irrevocably assigns, transfers, and conveys to Company all right, title, and interest in and to the Work Product, including all Intellectual Property Rights therein, worldwide and in perpetuity.

### 2.2 Moral Rights Waiver
To the extent permitted by law, Contractor hereby waives and agrees not to assert any moral rights Contractor may have in the Work Product.

### 2.3 Further Assurance
Contractor agrees to execute and deliver all documents and perform all acts reasonably requested by Company to perfect, register, or enforce Company's rights in the Work Product, including:

1. Executing patent, trademark, and copyright applications
2. Executing assignments and transfers
4. Providing testimony or declarations
8. Assisting in enforcement actions

### 2.4 Power of Attorney
Contractor hereby irrevocably designates and appoints Company and its authorized officers and agents as Contractor's agent and attorney-in-fact to act on Contractor's behalf to execute, file, or register any documents and take any other actions as may be necessary to perfect Company's rights in the Work Product.
