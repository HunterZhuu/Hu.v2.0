### Step 1: Form Your Business Entity (Week 1)
```

2. File with your state ($50-500)
3. Get EIN from IRS (free)
4. Open business bank account
```

```

```

```

```

## 🚨 Critical Rules

### Rule 1: Documents BEFORE Work
**NEVER** let anyone start work before all documents are signed:
- ❌ No code before IP Assignment signed
- ❌ No designs before IP Assignment signed
- ❌ No consulting before NDA signed
- ❌ No sharing before NDA signed

### Rule 2: NDA BEFORE Discussion
**NEVER** share project details before NDA is signed:
- ❌ No technical details before NDA
- ❌ No business model before NDA
- ❌ No financial projections before NDA
- ❌ No source code before NDA

### Rule 3: Business Entity BEFORE Everything
**NEVER** operate without a business entity:
- ❌ No contracts without business entity
- ❌ No IP ownership without business entity
- ❌ No legal protection without business entity

### Rule 4: Attorney Review BEFORE Signing
**NEVER** use legal documents without attorney review:
- ❌ No signing without attorney review
- ❌ No customization without attorney review
- ❌ No enforcement without attorney review
